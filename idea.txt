DROPLY: FEATURE IDEAS
=====================

Ideas to make Droply more fun and to give people reasons to come back. They're grouped by
theme, and each one is tagged with:

  Effort:  S = a few days, M = 1-2 weeks, L = several weeks
  Needs:   FE = frontend only, BE = backend change too, $ = running cost (storage, servers)

Ground rule: the promise is "fast, private, direct". Every idea below keeps file contents
off our servers unless the user explicitly opts in, and none of them sell or track what
people send.


STATUS (October 2026)
---------------------
Implemented: everything below except Language translations, Browser extension, Leave it
for later (encrypted cloud fallback) and the "Globetrotter" badge (the browser can't tell
which countries a relay crosses). Classroom mode is covered by event rooms (50 devices),
the delivery roster and normal sending back to the host.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TOP PICKS (best value for the effort, in order)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. My devices: remember your own phone and laptop so they find each other without a code.
 2. Nearby devices: see devices on the same network instantly, no code, no QR.
 3. Clipboard and text sharing: send a link, a note or a password with one tap.
 4. Install as an app (PWA) with the "Share to Droply" menu on phones.
 5. Fun device names and avatars ("Sneaky Otter", "Turbo Falcon").
 6. Transfer stats and badges ("You've moved 120 GB at 85 MB/s").
 7. Photo and video preview grid for received media.
 8. Sound and confetti on completion, with a "speed record" moment.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. COME-BACK FEATURES (habit and convenience)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

My devices / pairing                                            Effort: M   Needs: FE BE
  Pair your devices once, by scanning a QR code a single time. Afterwards, opening Droply on
  any of them shows the others ready to receive. No codes, no rooms to create. This turns
  Droply from a "sometimes" tool into the default way to move files between your own
  devices. Store a pairing key on each device, and have the backend match paired devices
  when they come online (it never sees files).

Nearby devices (same network)                                   Effort: M   Needs: BE
  Group devices that share a public IP (same Wi-Fi) into a "Nearby" list automatically,
  the way AirDrop shows people around you. One tap to send. Keep it opt-in and
  short-lived for privacy.

Install as an app (PWA) + Share Target                          Effort: S   Needs: FE
  Add a manifest and a service worker so Droply installs on the home screen and desktop.
  With the Web Share Target API it then appears in the phone's own "Share" menu, so you
  pick a photo in your gallery, tap Share → Droply, and it sends. This is the single
  biggest driver of repeat use on phones.

Clipboard and text drop                                         Effort: S   Needs: FE
  A "Send text" box next to the drop zone for links, notes, addresses and Wi-Fi passwords,
  plus "Paste from clipboard". Tiny messages can go over the DataChannel instantly. People
  move text between their phone and laptop many times a day.

Quick resend and history (stored only on your device)           Effort: S   Needs: FE
  A local list of recent transfers (names, sizes, devices, speed) with "Send again". Kept in
  IndexedDB on the device, clearable, never uploaded.

Saved rooms / "My room" link                                    Effort: M   Needs: BE
  A personal, permanent link (droply.app/r/solomon) that is always ready to receive, like a
  personal drop box. You share it once with friends and it opens a private room. The
  backend needs longer-lived rooms and owner tokens.

Browser notifications                                           Effort: S   Needs: FE
  "Mary wants to send you 3 files" as a system notification while the tab is in the
  background, using the Notifications API.

Keyboard power-user mode                                        Effort: S   Needs: FE
  Ctrl+V pastes files or text straight into a send, Ctrl+O selects files, and number keys
  pick recipients.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FUN AND PERSONALITY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Playful device names and avatars                                Effort: S   Needs: FE
  Assign each device a fun name and an emoji or animal avatar ("Sneaky Otter 🦦"), still
  editable. It makes rooms friendlier and devices easier to tell apart than "Windows
  Desktop" ×3.

Celebrations                                                    Effort: S   Needs: FE
  Confetti on a finished batch, a satisfying "whoosh" sound when sending, and a haptic buzz
  on phones (navigator.vibrate). All respect reduced-motion and a mute switch.

Live speed display while sending                                Effort: S   Needs: FE
  Already there: each transfer card shows the current speed and time remaining for both
  the sender and the receiver, and "Transfer details" adds the average speed, latency and
  connection type. Make it a feature people enjoy watching:
    - a big, readable speed number (e.g. "⚡ 87.4 MB/s") at the top of the card, not just
      small text under the bar
    - a speedometer-style gauge, and a small live graph of the last 30 seconds so you can
      watch it ramp up
    - a total speed for the whole batch when several files or recipients send at once
    - the speed per recipient when one file goes to several devices
    - a summary at the end: "2.8 GB in 35 s · average 79 MB/s · top 112 MB/s"
    - the connection type next to the speed ("⚡ Direct · 87 MB/s" vs "🔄 Relay · 9 MB/s"),
      so people understand why it's fast or slow
  Use only measured, verified bytes (no fake speeds), and update about 4 times a second.

Speed records                                                   Effort: S   Needs: FE
  "New personal record: 112 MB/s!" when you beat your best, with a small celebration.
  Stored locally.

Personal stats ("Droply Wrapped")                               Effort: M   Needs: FE
  "This month you sent 48 GB across 4 devices, mostly videos, fastest at 9 pm." A
  shareable, privacy-safe summary card, generated on the device from local history.

Badges / achievements                                           Effort: S   Needs: FE
  "First drop", "Gigabyte club" (1 GB in one go), "Marathon" (10 GB), "Speed demon" (over
  100 MB/s), "Party room" (5+ devices), "Globetrotter" (relay across countries). Local only,
  just for fun.

Room themes                                                     Effort: S   Needs: FE
  The host picks a color or theme for the room (birthday, wedding, gaming, study), with
  matching accent colors and a small banner.

Reactions                                                       Effort: S   Needs: FE BE
  The receiver can tap 👍 ❤️ 😂 on a finished transfer and the sender sees it. Sent as a tiny
  WebSocket or DataChannel message.

Animated "flight path"                                          Effort: S   Needs: FE
  On completion, a small animation of the file flying from the sender's device icon to the
  receiver's. It's the AirDrop magic moment.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SOCIAL AND GROUP USE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Party / event rooms                                             Effort: M   Needs: BE
  One QR code on a screen at an event, class or wedding: everyone joins, and the host
  broadcasts photos or slides to the whole room. Others can drop their photos in. Needs
  bigger rooms and a "host broadcasts" mode.

Shared photo album (live)                                       Effort: M   Needs: FE
  In a room, every received photo appears in a shared grid that everyone can save from.
  It's great after a trip or a party. Still peer-to-peer.

Room chat                                                       Effort: M   Needs: FE BE
  A small chat panel in the room ("sending the rest in a sec", "got it, thanks!"),
  delivered over the DataChannel between peers so the server doesn't store messages.

Invite by link, not just QR                                     Effort: S   Needs: FE
  "Share invite" uses the Web Share API, so the room link goes straight to WhatsApp,
  Telegram or iMessage from the phone's share sheet.

Classroom mode                                                  Effort: M   Needs: BE
  A teacher hands out a worksheet to 30 devices at once and students hand work back.
  Needs larger rooms and the host seeing who received what.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FILE-HANDLING FEATURES PEOPLE ASK FOR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Media preview                                                   Effort: M   Needs: FE
  Thumbnails for received images and videos, with a safe, sandboxed preview (raster images
  and video only, never HTML, SVG or executables).

Send a whole folder (keep structure)                            Effort: M   Needs: FE BE
  Keep folder paths, by sending the sanitized relative path over the DataChannel. The
  receiver saves into a chosen folder with the same structure, or as a zip.

Zip on the fly                                                  Effort: M   Needs: FE
  "Receive as .zip" for many small files, streamed while it's being written so it never
  sits in memory.

Image compression option                                        Effort: S   Needs: FE
  "Send smaller" for photos on slow connections: resize and compress in the browser before
  sending.

Leave it for later (encrypted cloud fallback, opt-in)           Effort: L   Needs: FE BE $
  The backend already supports Cloudflare R2. When the other device is offline, upload an
  end-to-end-encrypted copy, with the key kept in the link and never on the server, that
  expires in 24 hours. It shows clearly that this one isn't direct.

End-to-end encryption badge with a verification code            Effort: M   Needs: FE
  Show both users the same 4-emoji code ("🐙🚀🍕🌵") to confirm nobody is in the middle.
  The transfer schema already has an encryption field.

Scheduled / auto-send                                           Effort: M   Needs: FE
  "When my laptop comes online, send these" between paired devices.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GROWTH AND SHARING (getting new visitors)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Speed test page                                                 Effort: S   Needs: FE
  "How fast can your two devices talk?", built on the existing /dev/benchmark. People love
  speed tests and share the results. It's a good landing page for search.

Shareable result cards                                          Effort: S   Needs: FE
  "I just sent 4.2 GB in 51 seconds with Droply ⚡" as an image card to share.

No-account promise + transparency page                          Effort: S   Needs: FE
  A short, honest page showing exactly what the server sees (room code, device names, file
  names and sizes) and what it never sees (file contents). Trust brings people back.

Language translations                                           Effort: M   Needs: FE
  Start with the languages of your main users. All copy already lives in components and
  config, so it's easy to extract.

Browser extension                                               Effort: L   Needs: FE
  Right-click any image or link → "Send to my phone with Droply".


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. QUALITY OF LIFE (small things that make people stay)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Remember the last recipients and preselect them next time.                  Effort: S
  - "Keep this tab open" banner on phones while transferring.                   Effort: S
  - Show the estimated time before sending ("~40 s on this connection").        Effort: S
  - Drop files onto a device card to send to just that device.                  Effort: S
  - A "Room is about to expire, keep it open?" prompt.                          Effort: S
  - A full-screen QR code for scanning from across the room.                    Effort: S
  - Retry with a different route (force relay) when direct keeps failing.       Effort: S


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THINGS TO AVOID
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Accounts that are required: they kill the "open and send" magic. Make them optional
    at most.
  - Ads or trackers next to people's private files: they destroy trust.
  - Storing files on the server by default: it breaks the main promise and adds cost.
  - Too many notifications or celebrations: keep a mute switch and respect reduced motion.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUGGESTED ORDER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Phase A (quick wins, mostly frontend):
    PWA + Share Target, text and clipboard drop, fun names and avatars, celebrations,
    local history with "Send again", invite via the share sheet.

  Phase B (habit):
    My devices / pairing, nearby devices, browser notifications, stats and badges,
    media preview.

  Phase C (bigger bets):
    Party/event rooms and the shared album, room chat, folders and zip, end-to-end
    encryption verification, opt-in encrypted "leave it for later".
